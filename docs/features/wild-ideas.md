# Wild ideas

Ideas worth exploring later. These are brainstorms, not committed product plans.

## Fish panel

Treat a fish as its own object, separate from subsets. Let players create one by entering fish mode, choosing a digit, and selecting its candidate cells, or by selecting cells and choosing “Make fish.” Validate the selected cells against fish rules and show the base houses, cover houses, and eliminations.

A fish could also participate in chains through proven implications. An X-Wing exposes ordinary candidate links; larger fish may need grouped links. A Kraken Fish is a more advanced direction: combine a finned fish with chains that prove the same elimination for each possible fin.

## Uniqueness panel

Help players find and model uniqueness techniques such as Unique Rectangles and BUG+1. Start with a player selecting four cells and checking for a Unique Rectangle; show the deadly candidate pair, the candidates that break the pattern, and the resulting elimination. Expand to other uniqueness patterns later.

These techniques rely on the puzzle having a unique solution, so the panel should make that assumption clear and offer them only when it is appropriate.
