Use the `CoachCrustEdgeCountUnfinished` and `CoachCrustEdgeCountFinished` stories to review the published 9×9 Edge-Count layout.

## Sudoku Coach Entropic clues

`Board` renders authored L/M/H digit-order clues outside the indicated row or column and leaves a visible break where an Entropic path connection is forbidden. Their accessible outside-clue descriptions name the selected digit group, digits, house, and reading direction. Review the `CoachEntropicOrderUnfinished` and `CoachEntropicOrderFinished` stories for outside labels, and the `CoachEntropicSpiralUnfinished` and `CoachEntropicSpiralFinished` stories for broken path connections.
