## Aqre shade controls

`Board` provides shaded, unshaded, and clear mark controls for editable Aqre puzzles. Pass `aqreShades` and `onAqreShade` to enable them. Shade marks remain separate from digit entries and player colors.

## Sized Crust clues

`Board` renders Crust totals outside their row or column, edge-count values as smaller secondary labels (including zero), fixed region edges, and matching `A` tags for a disconnected region. Its accessible clue descriptions distinguish the total from the number of earlier region edges crossed. Use the `CoachCrustEdgeCountUnfinished` and `CoachCrustEdgeCountFinished` stories to review the published 9×9 Edge-Count layout.

## Sudoku Coach Entropic clues

`Board` renders authored L/M/H digit-order clues outside the indicated row or column and leaves a visible break where an Entropic path connection is forbidden. Their accessible outside-clue descriptions name the selected digit group, digits, house, and reading direction. Review the `CoachEntropicOrderUnfinished` and `CoachEntropicOrderFinished` stories for outside labels, and the `CoachEntropicSpiralUnfinished` and `CoachEntropicSpiralFinished` stories for broken path connections.
