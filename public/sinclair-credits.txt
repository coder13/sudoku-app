Sinclair Library and first playtest

The library contains 100 puzzles at each of 66 variant and difficulty choices.
The first playtest contains 230 additional puzzles. Sinclair compares the
order of deductions with published puzzles and credited player replays.
Gridroom grades each puzzle in the orientation that appears on the board.

Research sources

Nikoli and SakanaAI — handmade classic Sudoku
https://huggingface.co/datasets/SakanaAI/sudoku-bench-nikoli

Janko archive — original puzzle authors and replay solvers credited on each page
https://www.janko.at/Raetsel/Sudoku/index.htm
https://www.janko.at/Raetsel/Sudoku/Killer/index.htm

World Puzzle Federation Sudoku Grand Prix — competition test puzzles
https://www.janko.at/Raetsel/Sudoku/Grand-Prix/index.htm

Sudoku Exchange — independently generated comparison puzzles
https://github.com/grantm/sudoku-exchange-puzzle-bank

Some upper-level Classic puzzles use positions from the Enjoy Sudoku Players
Forum collection in Tdoku. Sinclair rearranges these positions, and sometimes
adds or exchanges starting digits. The library includes each underlying
completed grid once. The BSD 2-Clause notice for Tdoku is at /licenses/tdoku.txt.
https://github.com/t-dillon/tdoku/blob/af426180dc53aef89b82868e7b3fdfcf42165654/benchmarks/README.md
